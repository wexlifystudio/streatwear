Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Streat Wear ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website, including the live chat feature, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Your name and mobile number, when you start a live chat conversation with us or place an order.
  • The messages you send us through live chat or the order form.
  • Basic order details you submit, such as product, size, and delivery information.
  • Your email address, if you sign up for our newsletter.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your live chat messages and questions.
  • To process and confirm your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To send updates about new drops, if you've subscribed to our newsletter.
  • To improve our website and customer service.

How We Store & Protect Your Information

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, our database provider. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Only authorized Streat Wear staff can access your chat and order details, through our password-protected admin panel.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your live chat session, so you don't have to re-enter your details every time you visit. This information stays on your device and is used only to reconnect you to your own conversation.

Your Rights

You can ask us to delete your chat history or personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will remove your information unless we're required to keep it for legal or order-related reasons.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out to us on Instagram @streetwear_iz or through the live chat on our site.
